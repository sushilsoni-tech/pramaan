  • Approval events are assertions, not authenticated identity or proof of meaningful review.
  • Producer timestamps are not trusted timestamps.
  • Completeness is relative only to the included producer-supplied policy.
  • Verification does not establish truth, legality, correctness, or source authenticity.
  • An unpinned signature proves integrity against the included key, not the identity of its owner.
